Tiny Routes Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

The short version

Tiny Routes doesn't collect, sell or share your personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the game doesn't send anything over the internet.

What stays on your iPhone

The game saves your level progress, stars, coins, unlocked cosmetics, tutorial progress and settings on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This never leaves your device, except as part of your own iPhone backups. Deleting the app deletes it, and Settings → Reset Progress clears your level progress.

TestFlight and Apple

If you're testing through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports, device and usage details, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. That information is used only to find and fix problems in the game.

Children

Tiny Routes is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the update will appear on this page with a new date. Questions? Send feedback through TestFlight or email tinyroutesmedia@proton.me.
