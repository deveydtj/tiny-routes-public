Tiny Routes TestFlight

Install the Tiny Routes beta on your iPhone and send feedback through TestFlight.

Install the beta

  1. Install TestFlight on your iPhone.
  2. Open your Tiny Routes invitation on your iPhone.
  3. In TestFlight, accept the invitation and install Tiny Routes.

Send feedback

Open Tiny Routes in TestFlight and tap Send Beta Feedback. Include the level and what happened. Attach a screenshot if it helps.
